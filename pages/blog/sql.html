Revenue Aggregation with SQL

SQL filters, joins, and aggregates relational structured data directly within analytical databases.

Data Engineering Example

Join orders with customer profiles on customer IDs, then execute GROUP BY on order dates and customer segments to compute daily revenue metrics, verifying row counts before and after the join.

Implementation Notes

Standardize timezone offsets and revenue inclusion rules prior to aggregation. Ensure canceled or refunded orders are explicitly filtered according to business definitions.
